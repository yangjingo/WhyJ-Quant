# TSP market dashboard: field and producer audit

This audit describes the TSP market dashboard at revision [`f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc`](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/tree/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc). It is a source-code inventory, not a claim that WhyJ Quant reproduces the dashboard, that TickFlow supplies the aggregates, or that historical data and provider entitlements are complete. The [list-column catalog](./tsp-list-catalog.md) covers a different part of TSP; other pages and their fields remain unaudited.

The [dashboard page](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/frontend/src/pages/Dashboard.tsx) calls `GET /api/overview/market?as_of=...`; its [route](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/api/overview.py) invokes [`build_market_overview`](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/market_overview_builder.py), which loads enriched rows for the selected day and derives the aggregates. An omitted `as_of` resolves to the latest available day and may use live index quotes; an explicit date uses stored index history. The response date is a trading date, not the as-of instant of each constituent or a completeness guarantee. The page also obtains capability, quote status, data status and user preferences separately.

## Calculation and data-quality boundaries

- The builder filters rows when a volume column exists: it retains a row if `volume > 0` or its change is nonzero. It counts remaining rows as the denominator. Missing changes are treated as zero for up/down counts, while averages and the displayed median use only finite changes. The median is the sorted element at index `floor(n/2)`, including for even `n`. These details are necessary to reproduce breadth; they do not prove the input is a complete exchange universe.
- TSP's stock `change_pct` is treated as a fraction by its thresholds and [formatter](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/frontend/src/components/dashboard/shared.tsx): `0.03` is shown as 3%. Index `change_pct` is displayed directly as percentage points; historical index fallback multiplies by 100. Breadth and trend percentages, seal rate and high-volume percentage are also multiplied by 100. `turnover_rate >= 5` is TSP's high-turnover threshold; compare units before importing it. Currency, original amount/volume units, precision, revisions, finality and provider-specific price bases remain unverified.
- Strong counts use stock changes at least `+0.03` or at most `-0.03`; distribution uses eight explicit fractional bands. Amount total replaces missing amounts with zero and divides by all retained rows for the average. The `vol_ratio` average defaults to 1 when no finite values exist, whereas high-volume share counts `vol_ratio_5d >= 1.5` and divides by all retained rows.
- `limit_up` uses `signal_limit_up` or a positive consecutive count; `broken` and `limit_down` use their respective signals. If depth reports sealed readiness, false-sealed counts are subtracted from headline up/down counts. `seal_rate = limit_up / (limit_up + broken) * 100`, or zero if the denominator is zero. The response `failed` is set to zero; it is not independently established as an observed failure count. Tiers are separately grouped from consecutive counts, so do not assume their sum equals the depth-corrected headline.
- MA breadth counts rows with finite close and MA where close is at least MA, then divides by all retained rows, including rows without an MA. High/low counts use signal OR comparison with `high_60d`/`low_60d`. The configurable [emotion calculation](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/emotion_score.py) combines these and other aggregates; snapshotting only the final score cannot reconstruct the inputs and active preferences.
- Dimension membership comes from configured extension files. For timeseries files the builder selects the latest available extension date, not necessarily the requested `as_of`; an historical dashboard is therefore not evidence that the membership was known on that historical date. Rank rows with no finite changes are omitted; group count can exceed the count of finite changes used for the mean. A provider quote alone cannot reproduce memberships.
- An empty date returns zeros for several aggregates, an empty radar and neutral emotion score; other UI paths also replace missing values with zero or 50. Preserve missing, empty, stale and actual zero separately in any future WhyJ contract instead of treating display defaults as observations. `quote_status` and depth readiness do not certify that every row is live or final.

The market mainline calculation in `computeTspMarketMainline` uses fixed-date sector memberships and the market roster with dated limit-up streaks. It filters the original labels by membership size and an exact blacklist, then combines industry paths at two levels. Three observed limit-ups are required for a group to enter the comparison. Average percentile ranks of limit-up count, maximum consecutive boards, occupied rungs of at least two boards, and the count of stocks with at least two boards receive weights of 35%, 25%, 25% and 15%. It returns up to 30 groups. Leader selection uses streak length and turnover amount, and exact ties are resolved deterministically. Missing streak observations remain visible in the counts. [Pinned TSP market mainline](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/market_mainline.py)

## Delivery boundary

`computeTspMarketBreadth` calculates breadth, return distribution, trend, amount, activity and leaders for one fixed trading date. It requires an identified and versioned A-share universe, plus per-symbol observations with fractional returns, volume in shares, turnover in percentage points and amount in CNY. Duplicate symbols, dates outside the selected cross-section and observations outside the universe are rejected. Complete fixtures match the pinned dashboard's thresholds and upper median. Returns and proportions in the WhyJ result are fractions.

The calculation reports missing members, unresolved eligibility and observed zero-volume, zero-return exclusions separately. Eligible rows need a positive observed volume or a nonzero observed return. A field's numerator and denominator use eligible rows for which its input is available, so a missing return cannot increase the flat count and a missing MA cannot reduce trend breadth. Unavailable amounts and ratios remain nullable. Rankings use ascending symbol as the tie-breaker. The calculator uses only the supplied universe and preserves its revision for replay.

`computeTspSectorRanking` ranks the leading and lagging five groups in one dated industry or concept dataset. A membership spell runs from its inclusive effective start to its exclusive end and uses the latest revision known on the requested trading date. The result retains the universe and taxonomy revision, distinct group IDs, counts of eligible and observed members, return and amount denominators, and the highest valid-return stock. A true flat return can lead a declining sector; a missing return cannot. Groups with no comparable returns remain available for coverage inspection.

The `provider-dated-snapshot` basis describes membership reported for the exact requested trade date, including records captured after that date. It applies the same ranking formula and keeps each catalog and group membership revision, capture time and taxonomy. Coverage remains unknown. The snapshot basis describes historical provider records and carries no inferred publication or effective date for backtests. Effective-dated inputs are screened separately by their known-on and active dates. The result identifies WhyJ as the producer and records the pinned formula version.

`computeTspSectorRotation` builds the TSP return-rank rotation from independently calculated daily sector ranks. Each column sorts groups by mean member return, breaking ties by stable group ID. The window defaults to 12 comparable sessions and accepts 7–30. Each submitted session retains its universe and membership evidence, including sessions with zero comparable groups. `rankMovement` is the previous supplied session's rank minus the current rank, and stays null where that group has no comparable prior rank. The result identifies the WhyJ formula, sector taxonomy and membership basis. [Pinned TSP rotation](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/rps_rotation.py)

`computeTspMarketEmotion` returns the six pinned dimensions and a versioned composite when at least one active dimension has an observed term. It records the resolved thresholds, weights, normalization ranges, metric units and per-dimension observed term counts. A missing key skips the term. An explicit null or unusable range retains the pinned neutral fallback and contributes no observation count. An entirely unobserved snapshot has no composite score or label. [Pinned scoring](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/emotion_score.py)

`computeTspMarketRegime` calculates the four-dimensional environment state from profit, speculation, resilience and trend. Use fractions for returns and proportions, and integers for counts and streaks. The formula uses half-even rounding and returns the five pinned states. Every field identifies its observed value and normalization basis. Missing values use the pinned defaults when there is partial evidence, non-finite terms receive a neutral 50, and entirely unobserved input yields an unavailable state. An observed zero seal or MA20 breadth remains visible alongside the pinned 0.5 fallback. This state has its own formula identity alongside the dashboard's six-dimensional emotion score. [Pinned regime builder](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/regime_builder.py)

`computeTspMarketPhaseSeries` labels the supplied ladder sessions as ice, ignition, rally, climax, ebb or repair. It smooths maximum streak, first-board and second-or-higher widths, promotion and seal rates. The comparison uses the fifth earlier supplied session, or the first supplied session for a shorter prefix. The weak-state veto changes the daily candidate and a phase transition takes two matching candidates. The default `causal` mode leaves leading evidence gaps unavailable. The `retrospective-tsp` mode reproduces TSP's leading backfill and identifies fields sourced from later dates, while an entirely absent ladder yields no phase. [Pinned market phase](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/market_phase.py)

`computeTspMarketLadder` aggregates the supplied roster’s closing-limit streaks and adjacent prior-session evidence. It returns the observed tiers, up to five stocks per tier ordered by observed turnover amount, and exact height, widths and rung completeness when every roster member has a known streak. Promotion requires known prior eligibility and outcomes for a pool of at least ten. Seal rate uses closing-limit hits divided by closing-limit plus broken-limit hits. The reported ratio rounds to four decimals, and the regime input keeps the unrounded ratio. A fully observed zero-event pool has no measured seal rate and supplies the pinned `0.5` regime input separately. Preserve the exact prior exchange session and provider coverage when binding archive inputs. [Pinned ladder aggregation](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/market_phase.py)

`computeTspDashboardEmotion` derives those inputs from one dated roster, stock observations and the available changes of four core indices (`000001.SH`, `399001.SZ`, `399006.SZ`, `000680.SH`). The dashboard's advance, decline, strong-move and high-volume percentages divide by the full eligible row count. Mean and upper median stock returns use observed values. The ladder supplies closing-limit, seal and streak terms. A fully observed session with no limit events has a zero dashboard seal rate. Partial limit evidence remains explicit. Dated concept and second-level industry groups contribute their top three by return, with separate maxima for group average and coverage. WhyJ retains original group revisions, uses stable group labels to break score ties, and leaves a term unavailable when its input is entirely absent. [Pinned input assembly](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/market_overview_builder.py#L497)

`computeTspDepthSeal` checks best ask volume for up-limit candidates and best bid volume for down-limit candidates. Observed zero means sealed. The caller supplies dated candidates, provider timestamps, a cutoff and maximum observation age. Missing, off-date, stale and after-cutoff readings stay unavailable. The result subtracts observed unsealed candidates, reports the amount of missing evidence, and identifies the corrected count for the supplied scope. With optional Depth5 evidence, the emotion calculator uses that up-limit count in the headline and seal rate and retains the raw ladder and streaks. [Pinned Depth5 classification](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/depth_service.py#L242) · [Pinned dashboard correction](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/market_overview_builder.py#L433)

WhyJ Source already exposes selected TickFlow daily/minute bars, factors, snapshots and depth, and versioned bar/factor conversion; see [Source providers](./source-data-providers.md). These are inputs, not the aggregate formulas or a complete market universe. A future implementation needs explicit dataset and membership identity, observation time, price basis, units, algorithm/configuration version, source coverage, missing/finality and correction rules before persisting or syncing a dashboard value. TSP alerts and configured extensions must keep their own provenance and permissions. The remaining cross-panel inventory, contract and synchronization work is tracked in [#258](https://github.com/yangjingo/WhyJ-Quant/issues/258); provider-neutral migration is tracked in [#264](https://github.com/yangjingo/WhyJ-Quant/issues/264).

## Own-side Depth5 queue

`computeTspDepthQueue` takes exact up-limit and down-limit candidates and fresh same-date Depth5 snapshots. It selects the best bid volume for up-limit candidates and the best ask volume for down-limit candidates. Confirmed volume units yield shares and hands. Multiplying shares by the matching candidate's reference close yields CNY queue amount. Unknown units preserve the native measurement and leave normalized fields unavailable. A missing close affects the amount only. The optional warning threshold matches a positive queue at or below the specified cutoff. It uses each candidate's known side independently of the opposing-side seal result. [TSP Depth5 map](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/depth_service.py#L418) · [TSP ladder warning](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/strategy/monitor.py#L1652)

## Quote poll increments

`computeTspQuotePollDelta` compares an explicitly paired set of adjacent stock quote captures. The observations must belong to the same Shanghai continuous session and each symbol must have an advancing provider timestamp in that session. It reports raw cumulative counter differences, poll span and unavailable states for missing, stale, reset and session boundaries. A measured zero remains zero. A missing amount remains unavailable. Caller-confirmed volume and amount units enable conversion to hands and CNY. An optional threshold matches inclusively and requires a positive volume increment even when comparing amount. [TSP quote poll accumulator](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/quote_service.py#L1800) · [TSP threshold rule](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/strategy/monitor.py#L1549)

## Intraday sector monitor

`computeTspSectorMonitor` takes a fixed index identity or revision-pinned industry or concept roster and ordered same-day observations. An eligible stock sector needs at least five members and observed returns for 80 percent of them. The monitor retains average constituent return, coverage, count and leader when coverage is incomplete. A measured flat return contributes to coverage. Tied leaders use the ascending symbol as the stable tie-breaker.

Each window measures the change in return fraction over 1, 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes. The baseline is the latest valid observation on or before the cutoff and at most 90 seconds older. A threshold checks for a false-to-true crossing after the initial valid observation and honors a cooldown. Invalid coverage does not update the crossing state. [Pinned sector monitor](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/sector_monitor.py#L281) · [Pinned rule evaluation](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/strategy/monitor.py#L854)

## Index minute chart returns

The TSP index chart uses each minute's close divided by the previous session's close, minus one. The index page disables the average-price and price-limit lines. WhyJ's `computeTspIndexMinuteReturns` returns the raw timestamp paired with a fractional return, preserving zero and leaving the value null if the previous close is unavailable. The calculator uses ordered positive index prices. Runtime verifies the underlying session and provider evidence before passing the prior close. [TSP minute chart](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/frontend/src/components/charts/LightweightIntraday.tsx#L46)
