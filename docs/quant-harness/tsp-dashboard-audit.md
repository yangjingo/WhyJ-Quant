# TSP market dashboard: field and producer audit

This audit describes the TSP market dashboard at revision [`f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc`](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/tree/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc). It is a source-code inventory, not a claim that WhyJ Quant reproduces the dashboard, that TickFlow supplies the aggregates, or that historical data and provider entitlements are complete. The [list-column catalog](./tsp-list-catalog.md) covers a different part of TSP; other pages and their fields remain unaudited.

The [dashboard page](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/frontend/src/pages/Dashboard.tsx) calls `GET /api/overview/market?as_of=...`; its [route](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/api/overview.py) invokes [`build_market_overview`](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/market_overview_builder.py), which loads enriched rows for the selected day and derives the aggregates. An omitted `as_of` resolves to the latest available day and may use live index quotes; an explicit date uses stored index history. The response date is a trading date, not the as-of instant of each constituent or a completeness guarantee. The page also obtains capability, quote status, data status and user preferences separately.

## Calculation and data-quality boundaries

- The builder filters rows when a volume column exists: it retains a row if `volume > 0` or its change is nonzero. It counts remaining rows as the denominator. Missing changes are treated as zero for up/down counts, while averages and the displayed median use only finite changes. The median is the sorted element at index `floor(n/2)`, including for even `n`. These details are necessary to reproduce breadth; they do not prove the input is a complete exchange universe.
- TSP's stock `change_pct` is treated as a fraction by its thresholds and [formatter](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/frontend/src/components/dashboard/shared.tsx): `0.03` is shown as 3%. Index `change_pct` is displayed directly as percentage points; historical index fallback multiplies by 100. Breadth and trend percentages, seal rate and high-volume percentage are also multiplied by 100. `turnover_rate >= 5` is TSP's high-turnover threshold; compare units before importing it. Currency, original amount/volume units, precision, revisions, finality and provider-specific price bases remain unverified.
- Strong counts use stock changes at least `+0.03` or at most `-0.03`; distribution uses eight explicit fractional bands. Amount total replaces missing amounts with zero and divides by all retained rows for the average. The `vol_ratio` average defaults to 1 when no finite values exist, whereas high-volume share counts `vol_ratio_5d >= 1.5` and divides by all retained rows.
- `limit_up` uses `signal_limit_up` or a positive consecutive count; `broken` and `limit_down` use their respective signals. If depth reports sealed readiness, false-sealed counts are subtracted from headline up/down counts. `seal_rate = limit_up / (limit_up + broken) * 100`, or zero if the denominator is zero. The response `failed` is set to zero; it is not independently established as an observed failure count. Tiers are separately grouped from consecutive counts, so do not assume their sum equals the depth-corrected headline.
- MA breadth counts rows with finite close and MA where close is at least MA, then divides by all retained rows, including rows without an MA. High/low counts use signal OR comparison with `high_60d`/`low_60d`. The configurable [emotion calculation](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/emotion_score.py) combines these and other aggregates; snapshotting only the final score cannot reconstruct the inputs and active preferences.
- Dimension membership comes from configured extension files. For timeseries files the builder selects the latest available extension date, not necessarily the requested `as_of`; an historical dashboard is therefore not evidence that the membership was known on that historical date. Rank rows with no finite changes are omitted; group count can exceed the count of finite changes used for the mean. A provider quote alone cannot reproduce memberships.
- An empty date returns zeros for several aggregates, an empty radar and neutral emotion score; other UI paths also replace missing values with zero or 50. Preserve missing, empty, stale and actual zero separately in any future WhyJ contract instead of treating display defaults as observations. `quote_status` and depth readiness do not certify that every row is live or final.

## Delivery boundary

WhyJ Source already exposes selected TickFlow daily/minute bars, factors, snapshots and depth, and versioned bar/factor conversion; see [Source providers](./source-data-providers.md). These are inputs, not the aggregate formulas or a complete market universe. A future implementation needs explicit dataset and membership identity, observation time, price basis, units, algorithm/configuration version, source coverage, missing/finality and correction rules before persisting or syncing a dashboard value. TSP alerts and configured extensions must keep their own provenance and permissions. The remaining cross-panel inventory, contract and synchronization work is tracked in [#258](https://github.com/yangjingo/WhyJ-Quant/issues/258); provider-neutral migration is tracked in [#264](https://github.com/yangjingo/WhyJ-Quant/issues/264).
