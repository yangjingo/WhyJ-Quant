## Factor mining training evidence

The mining workbench presents factor scores, rank IC, coverage, turnover and a daily Spearman matrix with the count of usable dates for each pair. `computeTspFactorMiningScreen` uses a fixed training panel of explicitly supplied factor values and forward returns. The score is absolute IC information ratio times factor coverage divided by one plus directional quintile turnover. IR uses the population deviation of daily IC. Coverage counts finite factor observations even on rows without a forward return. Turnover compares consecutive nonempty directional quintile holdings. Sample counts distinguish unavailable evidence from the pinned zero-score and turnover defaults. [TSP training metric](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/backtest/mining_runtime.py#L1275)

The correlation matrix averages valid daily Spearman correlations after ranking each pair's comparable stocks. Missing or constant pairs remain unavailable, with zero observed dates. The diagonal counts dates with at least two factor observations. Pruning walks candidates in descending score, IR and coverage, then ascending turnover and factor ID. A pair at or beyond the absolute correlation threshold is represented by the already selected factor with the strongest relationship. This defines the training screen. Candidate search, held-out evaluation and publication have separate input and result contracts. [TSP correlation and pruning](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/backtest/mining.py#L460)

## Mining candidate out-of-sample report

`computeTspMiningCandidateReport` summarizes one candidate's fixed overall outer folds. Every fold identifies its training and test dates, test session count, evaluation kind and either measured values or a skip reason. The calculator rejects duplicate fold IDs and training windows that overlap their own test windows. Valid and skipped counts refer to the supplied folds.

The report uses the mean of observed fold returns and Sharpe ratios, the worst observed Sharpe and drawdown, and the sum of observed trade counts. The positive-return ratio divides strictly positive returns by folds with a return, including observed zeros in the denominator. Robust score is 60% median Sharpe plus 40% worst Sharpe. Each field has its own coverage count. Skipped folds contribute no measurements and a field with no measurement is null. Candidate selection, fold scheduling and matched execution require their own evidence. [TSP overall candidate report](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/backtest/mining_runtime.py#L1211) · [TSP robust objective](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/backtest/robust_optimizer.py#L62)

The report's metric eligibility requires at least two valid folds, a positive-return ratio of at least two thirds, mean Sharpe of at least 0.5, worst drawdown of at least −25%, and at least 60 trades. Exploratory runs are ineligible. Strict runs also require positive worst-fold Sharpe when measured. The strict rule follows the TSP publication service, which supplies worst-fold Sharpe to the gate. WhyJ reports the thresholds, result and reason codes based on the supplied folds. [TSP thresholds and reasons](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/backtest/mining.py#L198) · [TSP publication check](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/services/mining_candidates.py#L122)

## Strategy Guardian

`planTspStrategyGuardianCheck` uses a fixed exchange-session sequence and counts sessions strictly after the strategy baseline date. The defaults require 30 new sessions and split the fresh span into as many as four equal calendar segments with at least 14 days each. The planner reports insufficient new sessions and short calendar spans separately.

`evaluateTspStrategyGuardian` binds objective, trade count and drawdown observations to the exact planned segment dates. It compares the robust score against the baseline and evaluates the worst signed drawdown and observed trade count. Relative degradation is unavailable at a nonpositive baseline. Missing objective scores make the evidence incomplete and cap the verdict at watch. If every objective score is missing, there is no verdict. The versioned output includes the thresholds, inputs, and reason codes. [TSP Guardian](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/backtest/guardian.py) · [TSP segment aggregation](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/backend/app/backtest/guardian_runtime.py#L527)

## Extension schema

`/api/analysis-menus` identifies configured menus. A data menu selects its `data_source`, template, dimension and rank fields, detail and group columns, and sort. A column carries its field name, presentation label, number, percentage, amount, date or string kind, visibility, precision and optional count/average/sum/minimum/maximum. `/api/ext-data/{id}/rows` supplies the selected projection. Hidden fields remain part of the configured schema. The menu also supports an embedded URL template that delegates the page to another origin. [Menu types](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/frontend/src/lib/api.ts#L5584) · [Analysis detail](https://github.com/shy3130/tick-stock-panel/blob/f9cb383c5918c6a88a412f3ce43d59fe81b94ccc/frontend/src/components/ExtDimensionAnalysis.tsx#L158)

User state owns the menu and column choices. The configured publisher owns source values. TSP can aggregate groups over the returned rows. A WhyJ mapping requires discovery of dataset ID and version, value type, unit, frequency, effective and observed times, source identity, entitlement and optional field semantics. Presentation format alone leaves unit and history eligibility unknown. The [exchange proposal](./observation-exchange-proposal.md) supplies a versioned envelope to evaluate before implementing a dynamic adapter.
